Design notes

Overall concept: onboarding through a direct prompt to complete a single action. The user is notified that they have a task, and the call to action links them straight to it. Within the product, several other signifiers also promote the required task, such as banners and notifications.

Assumption: the skills self-evaluation is a crucial first step, and the most important of the four profile setup tasks.

Trade-off: the suggested next step is to find an upskilling opportunity through gigs. This could equally point towards a second profile information task, creating a career journey, and so on.

Measure: completion of the task by its deadline, and the accuracy of the personal rating compared with the peer and leader ratings.

Measure: a qualitative review of whether level description transparency improves rating accuracy, whether seeing the whole task increases completion, and how clear completion success makes users feel about the task.
